# Phase 11: Universe-wide research shortlist + price-zone visualization

- Candidate selection no longer reserves exactly one slot per price band. Top three are chosen across eligible companies, with a 24-stock diversified fundamental shortlist (minimum 3 per price band when available).
- Horizon priority changes the order of **research** candidates. It is not a return forecast or a trading recommendation.
- **How finalists were selected** shows up to 12 quality-checked candidates and why some were excluded.
- The historical price chart displays adjusted daily close, 20-day average, upper/lower two-standard-deviation bands, and conditional markers: green ▲ when close <= lower band and RSI <35; red ▼ when close >= upper band and RSI >65.
- No chart can reliably identify the future exact top or bottom. Markers are retrospective watch conditions, not actionable buy/sell orders. Trends can remain overbought/oversold for long periods.
- The six-agent and investment committee code is unchanged.
- The fundamental shortlist is **not** the entire universe; not all 500 companies receive fundamental verification. No guarantee of optimal picks.
- Chart uses yfinance historical data; API use may incur fees for AI reviews. No trades are placed.
